How to Install My App on Android

You can safely install a lightweight, app version of my website directly onto your Android device without using the Google Play Store. This gives you faster loading times and an app icon on your home screen for easy access.

📲 Get started

Via Google Chrome:

  1.  Open Google Chrome on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to my website URL.
  3. Look at the top-right corner of your Google Chrome browser window and tap the three vertical dots (⋮) to open the menu.
  4. Scroll down the menu list and tap "Install and create shortcut".
  5. A confirmation box will appear showing my app icon. Tap Install.

How to Open and Use the App

  • Once installed, Chrome will place my app icon directly onto your phone's home screen or inside your app drawer.
  • Tap the icon to launch the app. It will run in a dedicated, full-screen window without the browser address bar at the top, just like a traditional mobile app.
